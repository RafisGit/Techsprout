## 8. Session Isolation

1. **Cryptographic Key Separation:**
   - In `render.yaml`, both services declare `AUTH_SECRET` with `generateValue: true`. Render generates unique, non-shared secrets for each service.
2. **Cookie Attributes:**
   - Both production and staging enforce `secure: true`, `httpOnly: true`, and `sameSite: 'lax'` (verified in `identity.controller.ts` and `otp.controller.ts`).
3. **Cross-Session Authentication:**
   - Cryptographic signatures are distinct; a token signed by one environment will fail HMAC/JWT signature validation in the other with `401 Unauthorized`.

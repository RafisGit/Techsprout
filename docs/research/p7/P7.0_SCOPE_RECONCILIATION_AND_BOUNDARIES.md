## 1. Executive Summary

This document addresses a critical scope discrepancy identified during the architecture review of the initial draft report ([`docs/research/p7/P7_RESEARCH_AND_PLANNING_REPORT.md`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/research/p7/P7_RESEARCH_AND_PLANNING_REPORT.md)). 

The draft report unilaterally incorporated **Interactive Discussions and Hands-on Project Assignments** as a core work package (WP-P7-03) of Phase 7, labeling them as "deferred P6.4 work." However, inspection of the authoritative master roadmap confirms that **Discussions and Assignments were formally chartered as Milestone P6.4 of Phase 6**, and were never formally re-assigned to Phase 7 by senior engineering leadership.

Silently importing P6.4 scope into Phase 7 violates release governance, creates an unmanageable 33.5-day monolithic release, and conflates data engineering (telemetry/AI) with pedagogical workflow engineering (forums/grading). This document reconciles the discrepancy, evaluates the architectural and organizational impact, and provides a formal governance recommendation.

## 4. Reconciled Scope Proposal for Phase 7

To establish rigorous engineering discipline, TechSprout Phase 7 is proposed as a multi-stage initiative centered on **Data Telemetry, Intelligent Learning, and Marketplace Monetization**, with Discussions and Assignments formally isolated.

### 4.1 Strict In-Scope Boundaries for Phase 7 (Proposed)

1. **Track 1: Append-Only Learning Telemetry (`learning_events`)**
   - High-throughput append-only `learning_events` relational schema in PostgreSQL.
   - Secure ingestion endpoint (`POST /api/v1/learning/events` and `/api/v1/learning/events/beacon`) with strict enrollment verification and payload validation.
   - Ingestion buffering and throttling to protect Render PostgreSQL and Redis resources.
   - Automated 90-day retention and data-pruning worker for high-frequency video checkpoints.
2. **Track 2: Client Telemetry & Video Retention Analytics**
   - Client-side `useLearningTelemetry` hook in `VideoPlayer.tsx` with fixed 60s throttling.
   - Reliable exit tracking via `navigator.sendBeacon()` and `fetch(..., { keepalive: true })`.
   - Pedagogical video retention curves (10% interval watch distributions) and rewind hotspot computation.
   - Accessible SVG charts integrated into `/instructor/courses/[id]/analytics`.
3. **Track 3: AI Tutoring & Contextual Learning (Sub-Phase P7.2)**
   - Text transcript extraction from course lecture media.
   - Vector embeddings pipeline and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) evaluation.
   - In-classroom AI student chat assistant constrained strictly to syllabus context.
   - Access control ensuring students can only query enrolled course materials.
4. **Track 4: Instructor Monetization & Marketplace Ledger (Sub-Phase P7.3)**
   - Internal double-entry accounting ledger (`instructor_wallets`, `wallet_ledger_entries`).
   - Revenue split accounting (e.g. 80% instructor / 20% platform) triggered on completed enrollment.
   - Escrow hold period (e.g. 14 days) preventing premature payout during refund windows.
   - Instructor withdrawal request workflow and administrative approval/disbursement queue.
   - Strict preservation of the single-merchant SSLCommerz checkout model.

## 5. Formal Recommendations Regarding Discussions and Assignments (P6.4)

Senior engineering leadership is presented with two explicit, mutually exclusive paths to resolve the P6.4 boundary:

### Recommendation Option A (RECOMMENDED): Retain Milestone P6.4 as an Independent Release
- **Action:** Remove Discussions and Assignments from Phase 7 entirely. Charter Milestone P6.4 as a standalone release: **"Phase 6.4 — Interactive Discussions & Hands-on Project Assignments"**.
- **Rationale:** 
  1. Preserves the original P6 master roadmap baseline.
  2. Allows educational forum and grading features to be designed, implemented, and QA-verified independently without competing with data telemetry or AI infrastructure.
  3. Enables Phase 7 to proceed immediately as a focused data/telemetry milestone (**Phase 7.1**).

### Recommendation Option B: Formal Scope Change Request (SCR-P7-01) for Phased Reassignment
- **Action:** Formally dissolve Phase 6 at Milestone P6.3 closure. Issue **`SCR-P7-01`** signed by the Head of Product and Staff Architect to reallocate Discussions and Assignments into a dedicated, late sub-phase: **Phase 7.4 (Educational Interactions)**.
- **Rationale:** 
  1. If leadership wishes to declare Phase 6 permanently closed, this provides a transparent audit trail.
  2. Prevents Discussions and Assignments from polluting Phase 7.1 (Telemetry) or Phase 7.2 (AI).

> **Policy Invariant:**  
> Under NO circumstances should Discussions and Assignments be silently implemented inside Phase 7 without explicit selection and approval of Option A or Option B by Senior Leadership.

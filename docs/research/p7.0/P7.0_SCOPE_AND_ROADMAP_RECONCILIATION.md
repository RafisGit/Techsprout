## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

During the initial scoping of Phase 7 (Intelligent Learning, Telemetry & Platform Expansion), an architectural proposal bundled **Interactive Lesson Discussions (Q&A)** and **Hands-on Project Assignments** as a core candidate work package (**WP-P7-03**), designating them as "deferred P6.4 work."

However, a strict audit of the authoritative master roadmaps revealed a major governance and scope discrepancy:
1. In the approved Phase 6 Scope Baseline ([`docs/qa/p6.0-scope.md`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/qa/p6.0-scope.md)), Interactive Discussions and Project Assignments were formally chartered under **Milestone P6.4** ("SHOULD HAVE for Phase 6").
2. In the Phase 6.3 Architecture and Planning Package ([`docs/research/p6.3/P6.3_SCOPE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/research/p6.3/P6.3_SCOPE_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md)), high-frequency video telemetry and the append-only `learning_events` table were explicitly **deferred to Phase 7** to prevent write contention on Render Free PostgreSQL.
3. No approved Scope Change Request (SCR) or executive leadership sign-off previously authorized the unilateral transfer of P6.4 scope into Phase 7.

Silently importing P6.4 into Phase 7 would inflate the milestone into an unmanageable 33.5-day monolithic release, conflating streaming telemetry and LLM retrieval with social forum and file grading engineering.

**Approved Resolution:** On October 17, 2026, Senior Engineering Leadership formally approved **Option B** via [`SCR-P7-01`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/research/p7.0/SCR-P7-01_SCOPE_CHANGE_REQUEST.md). Phase 6 is closed as complete, and Discussions & Assignments are formally reassigned to a dedicated milestone: **Phase 7.4**. Phase 7.1 remains strictly limited to the Learning Telemetry Foundation.

## 5. Explicit Out-of-Scope Register for Phase 7

To protect the production stability and financial safety of the platform, the following capabilities are explicitly classified as **OUT OF SCOPE** for Phase 7:

1. **Multi-Merchant Gateway Routing:**  
   *Classification:* Strictly Out of Scope.  
   *Rationale:* Preserves the single-merchant SSLCommerz invariant. All student payments flow directly to the platform's merchant account.
2. **Real-Time Automated Gateway Splits:**  
   *Classification:* Strictly Out of Scope.  
   *Rationale:* SSLCommerz Bangladesh does not support automated split settlement to secondary merchant accounts. Instructor payouts must operate via an internal ledger with batch administrative disbursement.
3. **Live Video Streaming Servers (WebRTC / RTMP):**  
   *Classification:* Strictly Out of Scope.  
   *Rationale:* External live class meeting URLs (Zoom, Google Meet, YouTube Live) remain the platform standard. Hosting custom WebRTC streaming infrastructure introduces massive cloud cost and operational complexity.
4. **SCORM / LTI Packaging & Interoperability:**  
   *Classification:* Permanently Rejected.  
   *Rationale:* TechSprout is a modern cloud-native B2C/consumer LMS; enterprise SCORM packaging adds technical debt with zero target user demand.
5. **Recurring Billing & Student Subscriptions:**  
   *Classification:* Deferred to Phase 8+.  
   *Rationale:* The core transaction engine operates on one-time course purchases. Subscriptions require recurring dunning management, card-on-file tokenization, and complex refund policies.
6. **External Data Warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, ClickHouse):**  
   *Classification:* Strictly Out of Scope.  
   *Rationale:* Current platform catalog scale (11 courses, ~100 lessons, hundreds of students) is optimally served within PostgreSQL 16. External analytical warehouses introduce multi-cloud data synchronization pipelines and unnecessary SaaS bills.

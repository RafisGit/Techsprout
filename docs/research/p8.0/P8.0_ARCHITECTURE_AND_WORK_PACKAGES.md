## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

### 1.1 Problem Statement
TechSprout currently operates exclusively on a **one-time, per-course purchasing model** (implemented in Phase 5.5). While this transactional engine reliably processes individual course checkouts via SSLCommerz, it imposes significant commercial and architectural limitations:
1. **Lumpy Cash Flow & Low Student Lifetime Value (LTV):** High customer acquisition cost (CAC) is incurred for single course purchases. Students complete or abandon individual courses without continuous commercial engagement.
2. **Catalog Friction:** Students interested in cross-disciplinary learning (e.g., Full-Stack Web Development, Data Science, and UI/UX Design) face cumulative price barriers when purchasing separate courses independently.
3. **Absence of Recurring Commercial Foundation:** The platform possesses no automated subscription lifecycle engine, recurring tokenized gateway billing, automated dunning/retry mechanism, or subscription-level entitlement synchronization.
4. **Marketplace Revenue-Share Rigidity:** The marketplace financial ledger (`ledger_entries`, `instructor_wallets`) is tightly coupled to individual order line items (`order_items`). It lacks an accounting model to apportion recurring subscription pool revenue to instructors whose content is consumed by active members.

### 1.2 Proposed Phase 8 High-Level Scope
Phase 8 proposes introducing an enterprise-grade, regulatory-compliant **Recurring Subscriptions and Platform Memberships Subsystem** that coexists seamlessly with one-time course purchasing:
- **Subscription Plans:** Tiered memberships (e.g., *Monthly All-Access Pass*, *Annual Pro Membership*, and *Curated Career Track Subscriptions*).
- **Payment Mandates & Tokenization:** Secure, PCI-compliant storage of recurring payment mandates with the local gateway (SSLCommerz) and card-on-file tokenization.
- **Automated Lifecycle & Dunning:** Time-based billing cycle management, recurring invoice generation, grace-period handling, automated dunning retries via BullMQ, and self-service student cancellation.
- **Dynamic Entitlement Access Guards:** Fast, Redis-cached entitlement validation in classroom video players and course APIs that immediately grants or revokes catalog access based on real-time subscription status.
- **Double-Entry Recurring Accounting:** Transparent allocation of membership pool revenue to participating instructors while maintaining immutable double-entry ledger invariants.

## 2. Business Objectives, Boundaries & Success Metrics

### 2.1 In-Scope Capabilities (Proposed)
1. **Subscription Plan Catalog:** Administrative creation and management of subscription tiers with configurable billing intervals (Monthly, Annual), trial periods, and currency (`BDT`).
2. **Recurring Payment Mandate Tokenization:** Tokenized customer checkout via SSLCommerz Recurring API, storing provider tokens without storing raw cardholder data (PAN, CVV).
3. **Subscription Lifecycle State Machine:** Authoritative server-side management of states (`TRIALING`, `ACTIVE`, `PAST_DUE`, `CANCELED`, `UNPAID`, `EXPIRED`).
4. **Automated Dunning & Grace Periods:** 7-day grace period with 3 automated exponential-backoff billing retries on renewal failure before subscription termination.
5. **Subscription Entitlement Guard:** Dual authorization layer permitting course access via either direct lifetime enrollment OR active platform membership.
6. **Student Subscription Portal:** Student dashboard for viewing active plan status, renewal dates, past renewal invoices, updating payment methods, and self-service cancellation (cancel-at-period-end).
7. **Marketplace Subscription Revenue Pool:** Pro-rata monthly distribution of subscription net revenues to instructors based on verified student consumption (watch-time deciles from Phase 7.1 telemetry).
8. **Automated Financial Reconciliation:** Automated daily scanner detecting subscription billing discrepancies between internal ledgers and gateway settlement reports.

### 2.2 Explicit Out-of-Scope Boundaries (Strictly Excluded)
To preserve platform reliability and comply with prior architectural decisions, the following capabilities are strictly **EXCLUDED** from Phase 8:
1. **Multi-Merchant Gateway Routing:** Preserves the Single-Merchant SSLCommerz Invariant. All subscription revenues must flow into TechSprout's primary merchant account.
2. **Direct Bank Automated Debits (BEFTN / BACPS):** Batch bank account debiting without tokenized gateway mandates is prohibited due to high settlement latency and manual clearing risks.
3. **Metered / Usage-Based Cloud Billing:** Complex per-minute or per-API-call billing is excluded. Subscriptions remain flat-rate interval tiers.
4. **Enterprise Multi-Seat Organization Billing:** B2B corporate team accounts, consolidated invoicing, and centralized seat provisioning are deferred to Phase 9+.
5. **Physical Merchandise or Tangible Shipments:** Limited purely to digital learning access.

## 3. Current Architecture & Integration Baseline

### 3.1 Existing Transactional Purchasing (Phase 5.5 Foundation)
The current commerce engine is strictly transactional:
- **`orders` & `order_items`:** Store immutable pricing snapshots (`subtotal_cents`, `discount_cents`, `payable_cents`) in minor units (`integer`) with database checks ensuring non-negative values (`CHECK ("payable_cents" >= 0)`).
- **`payments`:** Tracks gateway transactions (`merchant_tran_id`, `val_id`, `bank_tran_id`, `amount_cents`, `status`). Validation occurs via direct server-to-server call to SSLCommerz (`ISSLCommerzClient.validateOrder()`).
- **`enrollments`:** Lifetime access model (`status: 'ACTIVE' | 'COMPLETED' | 'CANCELLED'`). A unique index `enrollments_student_course_uq` enforces one enrollment per student per course.

### 3.2 Marketplace Ledger & Monetization (Phase 7.3 Baseline)
The marketplace ledger engine implements strict double-entry accounting:
- **`instructor_wallets`:** Contains `available_balance_cents` and `pending_balance_cents` with an integrity check constraint `CHECK (available_balance_cents >= 0)`.
- **`ledger_entries`:** Append-only ledger recording debits and credits with explicit transaction references.
- **14-Day Escrow Hold Period:** Earnings from course purchases are held in escrow for 14 days to accommodate student refund windows before settling to available balances.

### 3.3 Required Architecture Adaptations for Subscriptions
To support subscriptions without corrupting existing transactional invariants, the architecture must decouple:
1. **Purchase Type vs. Access Type:** An enrollment must distinguish between `LIFETIME_PURCHASE` (one-time purchase) and `SUBSCRIPTION_INCLUDED` (revocable membership access).
2. **Recurring Invoice vs. One-Time Order:** Recurring renewals must generate recurring `subscription_invoices` without modifying historical course orders.
3. **Escrow & Refund Alignment:** One-time purchases grant a 14-day refund window. Subscriptions require distinct refund boundaries (e.g., initial 48-hour cooling-off period for new activations; zero refunds on automated renewals once the cycle starts).

## 4. Proposed Architecture & Data Model

### 4.1 Subscription Lifecycle State Machine

```text
               ┌───────────────────────┐
               │      INCOMPLETE       │
               └───────────┬───────────┘
                           │ Payment Verified / Mandate Saved
                           ▼
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │                      ACTIVE                      │◄───────────────────┐
  └────────────┬────────────────────────┬────────────┘                    │
               │ Renewal Fails          │ User Cancels                    │ Renewal Recovers
               ▼                        ▼                                 │
  ┌────────────────────────┐  ┌──────────────────────┐                    │
  │        PAST_DUE        │  │       CANCELED       │                    │
  │ (7-Day Dunning Period) │  │(Access until End-Date│                    │
  └────────────┬───────────┘  └──────────┬───────────┘                    │
               │                         │                                │
               │ Retries Exhausted       │ Period Ends                    │
               ▼                         ▼                                │
  ┌────────────────────────┐  ┌──────────────────────┐                    │
  │         UNPAID         │  │       EXPIRED        │                    │
  │  (Access Suspended)    │  │  (Access Terminated) ├────────────────────┘
  └────────────────────────┘  └──────────────────────┘
```

#### State Definitions:
- **`INCOMPLETE`:** Initial mandate initiated; awaiting gateway authorization and first cycle validation.
- **`ACTIVE`:** Valid mandate on file; current billing cycle fully paid; active course access granted.
- **`PAST_DUE`:** Scheduled renewal charge failed; student in 7-day grace period with active dunning retries; access preserved.
- **`CANCELED`:** Student or admin requested cancellation; subscription will not renew, but access continues until `current_period_end`.
- **`UNPAID`:** Dunning retries exhausted without successful payment; catalog access immediately suspended.
- **`EXPIRED`:** Subscription completed terminal period after cancellation; catalog access completely revoked.

### 4.3 Webhook Idempotency & Concurrency Controls
1. **Idempotency Keying:** Webhook events from SSLCommerz IPN dispatch must be deduplicated using composite unique keys (`merchantTranId`, `valId`).
2. **Distributed Mutex Locking:** When recurring renewal jobs or webhook events process renewals, a distributed lock must be acquired via Redis:
   ```typescript
   const lockKey = `lock:subscription:renewal:${subscriptionId}`;
   // TTL: 30 seconds; release upon transaction commit
   ```
3. **Database-Level Row Locking:** Critical billing updates must execute with row-level locks (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) within explicit serializable transactions to eliminate double-billing race conditions.

## 5. Security, Operational & Regulatory Requirements

### 5.1 PCI-DSS & Payment Token Boundaries
- **Zero Raw Cardholder Data:** TechSprout servers must never receive, log, process, or persist Primary Account Numbers (PAN), Card Verification Values (CVV), or card PINs.
- **Mandate Tokenization:** All recurring billing relies exclusively on multi-use gateway tokens (`gateway_token`) issued by SSLCommerz upon validated customer consent.
- **Masked Data Only:** The database may only store non-sensitive display artifacts: `card_brand` (e.g., VISA, Mastercard) and `masked_card_number` (e.g., `XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-4242`).

### 5.2 Regulatory Alignment (Bangladesh Bank Standing Orders)
- **Mandate Consent & 2FA:** Initial tokenization requires explicit two-factor authentication (OTP via SMS/email) per Bangladesh Bank electronic mandate regulations.
- **Notification Prior to Debit:** Students must receive an automated email notification at least 24 hours prior to scheduled recurring renewal charges.
- **Frictionless Revocation:** Students must be provided with an immediate, self-service cancellation option on their web dashboard without requiring administrative contact.

### 5.3 Auditability & Sentry Error Isolation
- Every subscription lifecycle state change (`CREATED`, `ACTIVATED`, `RENEWED`, `FAILED`, `CANCELED`, `REVOKED`) must record an authoritative audit entry via [`AuditService`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/apps/api/src/modules/audit/audit.service.ts).
- Webhook payloads must be scrubbed of any potential credential fields via `PaymentsService.sanitizeGatewayData()` before logging.

### 8.2 DEC-P8-02 Detailed Specification: Instructor Revenue-Share Model Evaluation

A comprehensive financial modeling, accounting reconciliation, adversarial sensitivity evaluation, and active-learner qualification semantics and fraud audit was executed and formalized in [`P8.0.3_INSTRUCTOR_REVENUE_SHARE_MODEL_EVALUATION.md`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/research/p8.0/P8.0.3_INSTRUCTOR_REVENUE_SHARE_MODEL_EVALUATION.md), [`scratch/evaluate-subscription-rev-share.mjs`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/scratch/evaluate-subscription-rev-share.mjs), and [`scratch/evaluate-qualification-semantics-audit.mjs`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/scratch/evaluate-qualification-semantics-audit.mjs):

1. **Confirmed Architectural & Financial Findings:**
   - **Double-Entry Zero-Sum Reconciliation:** Across all subscriber volume scenarios, both variable pool models (Model 1: Watch-Time and Model 2: Active Enrollments) reconcile to zero-sum precision down to the single integer BDT poisha using the **Hamilton-Hare Largest Remainder Method**, satisfying `ledger_entries` invariants.
   - **Telemetry Privacy Inconsistency & Ephemeral Retention:** The preliminary qualification rule (`watch_time >= 15 min OR completed_lessons >= 1`) contained a technical contradiction: watch time originates from `learning_events`, which rotates user pseudonyms daily at `00:00:00 UTC` using an in-memory HMAC-SHA256 salt (ADR-P7-001) and deletes rows older than 90 days (`TelemetryPruningWorker`). Cross-day student watch time cannot be correlated across a month without violating ADR-P7-001.
   - **Semantics Audit of `watch_position_seconds` (P8.0.3.2):** Code inspection of [`learning.service.ts`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/apps/api/src/modules/learning/learning.service.ts) and [`VideoPlayer.tsx`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/apps/web/src/components/learning/VideoPlayer.tsx) proved that `watch_position_seconds` is strictly a playback bookmark, not an accumulator of elapsed watch time. Students can seek directly to 900s in 0 seconds, seeking backward overwrites the stored value with a smaller number, reaching $\ge 95\%$ of duration resets the playhead to 0 (ADR-0005 5.2), and lessons under 15 minutes can never reach 900s. **The assumption that `watch_position_seconds >= 900` proves 15 minutes of engagement is empirically retracted.**
   - **Evaluation of Four Qualification Alternatives:**
     - **Alternative A (Pure Completion-Based):** Qualified if $\ge 1$ lesson is completed in billing month (`lesson_progress.completed_at IN month`). **100% immune to 90-day pruning, 100% compliant with ADR-P7-001**, single indexed SQL query over persistent relational tables.
     - **Alternative B (Telemetry Watch-Time Aggregation):** Qualified if $\ge 900\text{ s}$ watch time accumulated in month. **Fails under current architecture**: violates ADR-P7-001 daily pseudonymity, requires an unhashed daily tracking table, and data is lost after 90 days.
     - **Alternative C (Corrected Relational Hybrid):** Qualified if completed $\ge 1$ lesson in month OR accessed course in month with $\ge 2$ distinct lessons visited (`COUNT(DISTINCT lesson_id) >= 2`). **100% immune to 90-day pruning, 100% compliant with ADR-P7-001**, captures both course finishers and legitimate review learners using existing relational fields.
     - **Alternative D (Enrollment + Recency Gate):** Qualified if enrolled and `enrollments.last_accessed_at IN month`. **Fails fraud resistance**: highly vulnerable to trivial enrollment click-spam and bot link-crawling.
   - **Financial Invariance Across Defensible Alternatives:** Simulation on the 100-subscriber cohort ($32,401.25\text{ BDT}$ pool) confirmed that Alternative A and Corrected Alternative C distribute revenue within 1.0% variance of each other (Web Dev: 46.8%-47.6%, Data Science: 28.2%-28.6%, UI/UX: 17.1%-17.7%, DevOps: 6.7%-7.3%), proving that eliminating telemetry watch-time tracking does not distort instructor compensation.
   - **Bot Vulnerability (Model 1 vs Model 2):** In simulation, a 10-bot video loop attack captured 98.7% of the platform pool under Model 1. Under Model 2 (Alt A or Corrected Alt C), each bot account represents at most 1 unique learner, blunting bot effectiveness by over 78%.
   - **Platform Solvency & Cash-Flow Risk (Model 3: Retainers):** Fixed retainers ($36,000\text{ BDT/month}$ across 4 instructors) expose the platform to severe cash deficits under low subscriber volumes ($-31,125\text{ BDT}$ deficit at 10 subscribers; break-even requires $\ge 75$ subscribers under corrected gateway charges).

2. **Evaluator Recommendation:**
   - **Model 2 (Threshold-Gated Active Enrollments with Alternative A or Corrected Alternative C)** is technically recommended as the baseline for Phase 8 because it is 100% immune to 90-day telemetry pruning, operates strictly on persistent ACID relational data (`enrollments` and `lesson_progress`), complies with ADR-P7-001 without schema modifications, preserves zero solvency risk, and delivers high anti-gaming resistance.

3. **Decisions Still Required from Leadership:**

### 8.3 DEC-P8-03 Detailed Specification: Subscription Cancellation, Refund & Financial Policy Evaluation

A comprehensive comparative policy evaluation, regulatory and provider compatibility audit, and integer-poisha financial simulation was conducted and formalized in [`P8.0.4_SUBSCRIPTION_CANCELLATION_AND_REFUND_POLICY_EVALUATION.md`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/research/p8.0/P8.0.4_SUBSCRIPTION_CANCELLATION_AND_REFUND_POLICY_EVALUATION.md) and [`scratch/evaluate-subscription-cancellation-refunds.mjs`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/scratch/evaluate-subscription-cancellation-refunds.mjs):

1. **Existing Platform Baseline & Non-Reusability:**
   - The existing refund engine (`apps/api/src/modules/refunds/refund-requests.service.ts`) is designed exclusively for single one-time course orders (`item_type = COURSE`), enforcing a 7-day request window and `< 20%` individual course progress.
   - Subscriptions provide catalog-wide entitlements across multiple courses; progress cannot be evaluated against an entire dynamic catalog.
   - Subscriptions pool revenues across multiple instructors (`SubscriptionRevenueService`), meaning any post-settlement refund cannot be clawed back from individual instructor wallets without triggering negative balance invariant violations (`CHECK (available_balance_cents >= 0)`) or breaching instructor trust.
   - Subscription cancellation, cooling-off refunds, and dunning lifecycle state transitions require separate, purpose-built subscription services (`SubscriptionLifecycleService`, `SubscriptionRefundService`).

2. **Verified Authoritative External Requirements:**
   - **The Consumers' Right Protection Act, 2009 (ভোক্তা অধিকার সংরক্ষণ আইন, ২০০৯ — Act No. 26 of 2009):**
     - Sections 37, 40, 44, 45, and 53 prohibit price inflation, deceptive advertising, and service failure.
     - The Act does **not** mandate a statutory right of withdrawal or cooling-off period for digital streaming services or SaaS memberships. Any cooling-off period is a **contractual product policy**.
     - Once published, policy terms are legally binding under Sections 44 and 45; arbitrary refusal to honor published refund terms constitutes an actionable statutory offense.
   - **Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines, 2021 (ডিজিটাল কমার্স পরিচালনা নির্দেশিকা ২০২১ — Gazette Notification No. 26.00.0000.112.014.004.2021-182):**
     - *Clause 3.1.4 & 3.1.5:* Platform credentials, terms and conditions, refund policies, product specifications, and total prices must be clearly disclosed prior to payment authorization.
     - *Clause 3.1.9:* Digital wallets, gift cards, or cash vouchers cannot be issued or used without Bangladesh Bank approval. Trapping customer refunds in captive platform credits is legally prohibited.
     - *Clauses 3.3.4, 3.3.6 & Section 3.5:* Approved refunds must be disbursed within **a maximum of 10 calendar days** via the **exact same payment medium/channel** (Card, bKash, Nagad) through which the student originally paid.
   - **SSLCommerz Payment Gateway Constraints & Sunk Fees:**
     - The SSLCommerz Refund API (`merchantTransIDvalidationAPI.php` with `refund_amount`) processes reversals back to the originating funding source.
     - Under Bangladesh tax regulations (VAT Act 2012), gateway commissions are subject to 15% VAT. On a ৳500 charge, the 2.875% effective fee is **৳14.38 (1,438 poisha: ৳12.50 base MDR + ৳1.88 VAT)**.
     - **Contractual Status:** TechSprout's specific contractual MDR schedule with SSLCommerz is an **unconfirmed scenario assumption** awaiting formal written addendum approval.
     - **Non-Refundability:** Gateway MDR fees and VAT are **non-refundable** upon customer refund. The platform permanently absorbs a ৳14.38 sunk cash loss per full refund.
     - Chargeback penalties: Contested card renewals incur 1,000–2,500 BDT in non-refundable dispute administration fees. Maintaining a chargeback ratio $> 0.9\%$ risks merchant account suspension.

3. **Core Policy Options Evaluated:**
   - **Cancellation Models:**
     - *Option A (Cancel-At-Period-End):* Student maintains full catalog access until `current_period_end`. No mid-cycle prorated refunds. Zero ledger adjustments mid-cycle. Clean, standard SaaS UX. Self-service one-click resume supported prior to expiration.
     - *Option B (Immediate Cancellation with Proration):* Immediate access revocation with daily/hourly proration. Rejected due to micro-transaction gateway fee losses, accounting pool instability, and complex fractional poisha math.
   - **Initial Cooling-Off Window:**
     - *Option A (48-Hour Capped Cooling-Off):* 48 hours from initial activation, $\le 2$ lessons completed across catalog, 0 certificates generated. 100% full refund (৳500.00). Platform absorbs the non-refundable ৳14.38 gateway fee as a customer acquisition cost (CAC). Instantly revokes entitlement.
     - *Option B (Strict Zero-Refund Policy):* High dispute rate, customer friction, potential consumer protection complaints.
   - **Recurring Renewal Refund Policy:**
     - *Option A (Controlled 48-Hour Unused Exception):* Zero refunds on renewals by default, with a strict automated exception: if requested within 48 hours of renewal charge AND zero lessons were accessed in the renewed billing period (`COUNT(lessons_accessed) = 0`). Full refund issued; platform absorbs ৳14.38 fee.
     - *Option B (Blanket No-Refund Enforcement):* Leads directly to bank chargebacks. Financial simulation proved that for 1,000 renewals with 10 complaints (4 resulting in chargebacks), Option B loses **-8,000.00 BDT** in chargeback penalties and lost fees, whereas Option A costs only **-115.04 BDT** in sunk gateway charges (8 x ৳14.38), saving **7,884.96 BDT** and preserving a 0.00% chargeback ratio.
     - *Defect/Duplicate Policy:* Automated 100% refund without condition for gateway duplicates or system billing glitches.
   - **Payment Failure & Dunning Strategy:**
     - 5-day grace period with 4 smart retry attempts (Day 0, Day 1, Day 3, Day 5) preserving active access. Simulation showed 75% payment recovery rate, recovering 1,500.00 BDT out of 2,000.00 BDT failed renewals.
     - Access suspended (`PAST_DUE_SUSPENDED`) immediately upon 4th failed attempt on Day 5. Permanent expiration (`EXPIRED`) on Day 14.

4. **Financial Reconciliation, Ledger Invariants & Post-Settlement Protections:**
   - Integer minor-unit poisha balancing verified across all scenarios (`scratch/evaluate-subscription-cancellation-refunds.mjs`).
   - **Direct Instructor Wallet Clawbacks are REJECTED:** If a renewal refund or chargeback occurs after monthly pool settlement, clawing back instructor wallets risks violating `CHECK (available_balance_cents >= 0)` if funds were withdrawn, creating negative balances and operational friction.
   - **Platform Subscription Reserve Account:** The platform retains 5% of its retained commission into an internal `SUBSCRIPTION_RESERVE_ACCOUNT` (৳688.41/month per 100 subscribers; ৳8,260.92/year), which absorbs all post-settlement chargebacks and dispute fees without touching settled instructor earnings.

5. **Evaluator Recommended Policy Package:**
   - **Cancellation:** Cancel-at-period-end with full access through cycle, zero mid-cycle proration, self-service one-click resume.
   - **Initial Cooling-Off:** 48-hour window from checkout, $\le 2$ lessons completed, 0 certificates, 100% refund, platform absorbs ৳14.38 gateway fee.
   - **Renewals:** Strict no-refund general rule with 48-hour unused exception ($0$ lessons accessed) and 100% automated refund on duplicate debits.
   - **Dunning:** 5-day grace period, 4 retry attempts, instant suspension on Day 5 failure, expiration on Day 14.
   - **Ledger & Reserve:** 5% platform commission reserve allocation to insulate instructor wallets from post-settlement adjustments.

6. **Decisions Still Required from Leadership:**
   - **Product:** Approval of the 48-hour cooling-off window and $\le 2$ lesson completion ceiling.

   - **Legal:** Approval of Terms of Service wording for digital content waiver and 48-hour renewal exception under Bangladesh Digital Commerce Guidelines 2021.
   - **Customer Operations:** SLA confirmation for processing refund exception tickets within the statutory 10-day refund disbursement window.

### 9.1 SSLCommerz Recurring Payments & Tokenization Feasibility

#### Documented Gateway Architecture vs. Unverified Capabilities
Inspection of the existing integration ([`sslcommerz.client.ts`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/apps/api/src/modules/payments/sslcommerz.client.ts)) and official documentation from SSLCommerz (`developer.sslcommerz.com`) establishes the exact baseline:

1. **Documented Standard Integration (Active in TechSprout):**
   - **Session Initiation:** Server-to-server POST to `gwprocess/v4/api.php` generating a `GatewayPageURL` (`ISSLCommerzClient.initiateSession()`).
   - **Consumer Redirect:** Browser navigates to hosted gateway checkout; customer selects payment channel (Visa, Mastercard, bKash, Nagad) and completes interactive 3D Secure OTP verification.
   - **Validation & IPN:** Asynchronous IPN POST dispatched to server; backend invokes Order Validation API (`validator/api/validationserverAPI.php` with `val_id`) to verify payment status, card brand, and amount in minor units.
2. **Card Tokenization (Vaulting):**
   - *Documented Reality:* SSLCommerz maintains a PCI-DSS compliant tokenization engine capable of vaulting credit and debit card profiles to prevent customers from manually re-entering credentials.
   - *Contractual Restriction:* Tokenization and recurring debits are **disabled by default** on standard merchant profiles. Activation requires submitting a formal commercial request to SSLCommerz merchant operations (`support@sslcommerz.com`) to enable the tokenization feature flag for the TechSprout `store_id`.
3. **Recurring Standing Orders & Merchant-Initiated Transactions (MIT):**
   - *Regulatory Context (Bangladesh Bank):* Recurring debits on domestic cards are governed by central bank e-mandate directives. Initial tokenization requires explicit customer mandate consent with two-factor authentication (SMS/Email OTP).
   - *Provider Uncertainty:* While tokenization for repeat checkout (customer-present "One-Click Pay") is fully documented, automated headless server-to-server recurring billing (customer-absent merchant-initiated renewal debits) requires custom recurring payload parameters (e.g. `user_refer`, token identifiers) and specific acquiring bank agreements in Bangladesh.
   - *Alternative Local Recurring Rail (bKash Tokenized Checkout):* bKash offers a dedicated *Tokenized Checkout Agreement API* where users approve a recurring mandate once, allowing merchants to programmatically execute renewal debits via `POST /tokenized/checkout/execute`. This represents a proven contingency if card tokenization encounters regulatory OTP friction.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **FEASIBILITY VERDICT (GATEWAY):** Card tokenization is technically supported by the gateway provider, but headless automated recurring debit capability **requires written merchant account confirmation and technical addendum documentation from SSLCommerz** before implementation can proceed. A comprehensive written merchant inquiry draft and 9-point verification checklist have been initialized in [`P8.0_SSLCOMMERZ_MERCHANT_INQUIRY_AND_CHECKLIST.md`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/research/p8.0/P8.0_SSLCOMMERZ_MERCHANT_INQUIRY_AND_CHECKLIST.md) awaiting authorized transmission.

### 9.3 Refund, Cancellation & Lifecycle State Machine Feasibility

#### Technical State Transitions & Timing Requirements
The existing refund engine ([`apps/api/src/modules/refunds/`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/apps/api/src/modules/refunds/)) is tailored for single orders with a 14-day window and manual admin approval. Subscription lifecycles require a distinct, deterministic model:

1. **Cancellation Dynamics:**
   - **Cancel-At-Period-End (Standard SaaS Invariant):** When a user requests cancellation via the dashboard, status changes to `CANCELED` with `cancel_at_period_end = true`.
   - *Technical Timing:* The subscription remains entitled to full catalog access until `current_period_end`.
   - *Accounting Consequence:* Zero financial reversals, zero refund API calls, zero ledger modifications. On the exact timestamp of `current_period_end`, a BullMQ worker transitions status to `EXPIRED` and clears the Redis entitlement cache.
2. **Initial Activation Cooling-Off Window (Proposed Policy A):**
   - *Timing Window:* Within 48 hours of initial subscription activation.
   - *Eligibility Gate:* Total watch time $< 30\text{ minutes}$ across the catalog.
   - *Technical Execution:*
     1. Dispatches automated refund request via `ISSLCommerzClient.initiateRefund()` with original `val_id` and `amount_cents`.
     2. Transitions subscription status directly to `REFUNDED`.
     3. Instantly revokes Redis entitlement cache key `entitlement:sub:${studentId}`.
     4. Records an offsetting debit to platform subscription escrow in `ledger_entries`.
     5. Note on Gateway Fees: SSLCommerz transaction processing fees ($\sim 2.0\%–2.5\%$) are non-refundable by the gateway; policy must declare whether platform absorbs this cost or deducts it from the refund amount.
3. **Recurring Renewal Cycles:**
   - Automated renewal charges are **strictly non-refundable** once processed, except under proven gateway double-charge defects identified by the reconciliation scanner.

## 1. Context & Business Motivation

TechSprout has successfully validated its core educational delivery, single-course SSLCommerz transactions (Phase 5.5), and learning analytics foundation (Phase 7.1). However, the business operates under significant commercial constraints due to its exclusive reliance on one-time course sales:

1. **Revenue Volatility:** Course revenue fluctuates heavily based on promotional campaigns and seasonal enrollment cycles.
2. **Student Acquisition Inefficiency:** Student lifetime value (LTV) is capped when students purchase only one course despite an expanding catalog.
3. **Prior Roadmap Directive:** In Phase 7 scoping ([`P7.0_SCOPE_AND_ROADMAP_RECONCILIATION.md`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/research/p7.0/P7.0_SCOPE_AND_ROADMAP_RECONCILIATION.md#L148)), recurring billing and memberships were explicitly identified as high-value capabilities and formally scheduled as the primary candidate for **Phase 8+**.

Phase 8 is proposed to introduce a recurring subscription engine that creates predictable Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), lowers learning barriers for multi-course students, and introduces subscription pool revenue share for marketplace instructors.

## 2. Options Considered

### Option 1: Full Tokenized Recurring Subscriptions & Memberships (Recommended Proposal)
- **Architecture:** Tokenized card-on-file payment mandates via SSLCommerz Recurring API; automated nightly billing workers via BullMQ; 7-day grace period with 3-attempt exponential dunning; dynamic dual-entitlement access guards.
- **Advantages:** Continuous recurring revenue; seamless student experience without manual monthly re-checkout; industry-standard SaaS metrics (MRR, churn, LTV).
- **Disadvantages:** Depends on gateway recurring mandate reliability in Bangladesh; potential regulatory OTP friction under Bangladesh Bank e-mandate rules; requires background worker infrastructure.

### Option 2: Pre-Paid Multi-Month Access Passes (Fallback Alternative)
- **Architecture:** Fixed-duration membership passes (e.g., 30-Day, 90-Day, or 365-Day Access Passes) purchased as standard one-time transactions using the existing Phase 5.5 checkout flow. When the pass expires, access ceases unless the student manually purchases a renewal.
- **Advantages:** Zero new gateway integrations; zero recurring tokenization risks; 100% supported by current SSLCommerz integration; zero regulatory standing-order friction.
- **Disadvantages:** Significantly lower retention and renewal rates due to manual re-checkout friction; does not achieve true automated SaaS recurring cash flow.

### Option 3: Status Quo / Defer Subscriptions Beyond Phase 8
- **Architecture:** Retain pure one-time course sales; focus engineering resources on international expansion or mobile native applications.
- **Advantages:** Zero added financial or billing complexity.
- **Disadvantages:** Fails to address student LTV caps and catalog cross-selling barriers; leaves platform vulnerable to competitors offering subscription-based access.

## 3. Recommended Approach & Architectural Safeguards

The proposal recommends **Option 1 (Full Tokenized Recurring Subscriptions)** as the primary target, with **Option 2 (Pre-Paid Passes)** documented as an immediate operational contingency if gateway recurring mandate capabilities encounter regulatory delays.

### Core Architectural Safeguards & Invariants Preserved:
1. **Single-Merchant Gateway Invariant:** All subscription billing flows directly to TechSprout's central SSLCommerz merchant account. Zero multi-merchant routing.
2. **Strict Double-Entry Ledger Balancing:** Subscription revenues and instructor pool allocations must balance to zero-sum precision via `ledger_entries`, protected by `CHECK (available_balance_cents >= 0)`.
3. **Decoupled Entitlements:** Lifetime course purchases are permanently recorded in `enrollments` and are completely insulated from subscription expiration or cancellation.
4. **Zero Raw Cardholder Data:** Strict adherence to PCI-DSS boundaries. Zero storage of PAN or CVV; all recurring charges utilize gateway tokens (`payment_mandates.gateway_token`).
5. **Decoupled Out-of-Band Migrations:** Subscription schemas (proposed Migration `0012`) must be applied via isolated out-of-band tasks with zero migration-on-boot overhead.

## 4. Sourced Technical Feasibility & Operational Evidence Summary (WP-P8-0.1)

In accordance with the Phase 8 Feasibility Verification mandate, the technical, operational, and architectural assumptions in the proposal have been audited against current official documentation, the existing codebase, and live infrastructure constraints:

### 4.1 Payment Gateway Recurring Capabilities (SSLCommerz)
- **Documented Baseline:** The existing client ([`sslcommerz.client.ts`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/apps/api/src/modules/payments/sslcommerz.client.ts)) implements standard one-time session initiation (`gwprocess/v4/api.php`), IPN validation, and Order Validation (`validationserverAPI.php` with `val_id`).
- **Tokenization (Vaulting):** SSLCommerz supports PCI-DSS compliant card tokenization to store card profiles, but it is **disabled by default on merchant profiles** and requires explicit written activation via `support@sslcommerz.com`.
- **Automated Recurring Debits:** In Bangladesh, central bank e-mandate regulations require interactive 2FA (SMS OTP) for initial mandate consent. Headless automated server-to-server renewal debits (without customer interaction) require written merchant account authorization, custom payload parameters (`user_refer`), and partner acquiring bank support.
- **Local Alternative Rail:** bKash offers an official *Tokenized Checkout Agreement API* where users approve a recurring mandate once, allowing merchants to programmatically execute renewal debits via `POST /tokenized/checkout/execute`. This represents a proven contingency if card tokenization encounters regulatory OTP friction.
- **Provider Due Diligence Artifact:** A complete written merchant inquiry draft and 9-point verification checklist have been prepared in [`P8.0_SSLCOMMERZ_MERCHANT_INQUIRY_AND_CHECKLIST.md`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/research/p8.0/P8.0_SSLCOMMERZ_MERCHANT_INQUIRY_AND_CHECKLIST.md) awaiting authorized transmission.
- **Feasibility Verdict:** Card tokenization is technically supported, but automated recurring renewals require written provider confirmation and account provisioning before implementation can proceed.

### 4.2 Instructor Revenue Share Allocation (Ledger & Telemetry)
A comparative evaluation was performed across the three candidate revenue-sharing models against the platform's double-entry ledger (`ledger_entries`, `instructor_wallets`) and telemetry pipeline ([`telemetry.ts`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/apps/api/src/database/schema/telemetry.ts)):
- **Model A (Watch-Time Deciles):** Sourced from `learning_events` (`VIDEO_CHECKPOINT`). Compatible with daily rotating salts (aggregations group by `course_id`), but tightly coupled to monthly aggregation before the 90-day pruning worker deletes checkpoint rows. Highly vulnerable to automated video-looping bots unless guarded by playback velocity caps and single-tab constraints.
- **Model B (Threshold-Gated Active Enrollments):** Sourced from `enrollments` and verified engagement (e.g., $\ge 15\text{ minutes}$ or 1 completed lesson in the billing cycle). Completely independent of telemetry salt rotation and ephemeral checkpoint retention. Lowest operational complexity and highest anti-gaming resilience.
- **Model C (Flat Retainers):** Fixed BDT monthly content license fees. Zero data or telemetry dependencies, but creates significant financial risk for the platform during early subscription ramp-up by committing to fixed liabilities regardless of subscriber volume.
- **Feasibility Verdict:** Model B (Threshold-Gated Active Enrollments) offers the best balance of operational simplicity, fraud resistance, and ledger safety. Model A is feasible with playback velocity guards. Model C carries substantial commercial downside. Full financial modeling and simulation evidence is documented in [`P8.0.3_INSTRUCTOR_REVENUE_SHARE_MODEL_EVALUATION.md`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/research/p8.0/P8.0.3_INSTRUCTOR_REVENUE_SHARE_MODEL_EVALUATION.md).

### 4.3 Refund & Cancellation Policy State Machine
- **Cancellation State Flow:** Standard SaaS cancel-at-period-end invariant. When a student cancels, status transitions to `CANCELED` with `cancel_at_period_end = true`. Access persists until `current_period_end` with zero financial reversals or ledger adjustments. On period end, status transitions to `EXPIRED` and the Redis entitlement cache is cleared.
- **Initial Cooling-Off Window (Recommended 48 Hours):** If a student requests a refund within 48 hours of initial activation and has watched $< 30\text{ minutes}$ of content, the system triggers `ISSLCommerzClient.initiateRefund()`, immediately revokes the entitlement cache key, transitions status to `REFUNDED`, and posts an offsetting debit to platform subscription escrow in `ledger_entries`.
- **Gateway Fee Accounting:** Gateway transaction fees ($\sim 2.0\%–2.5\%$) are non-refundable by SSLCommerz; policy must explicitly determine whether TechSprout absorbs these fees or deducts them from the refunded amount.
- **Renewals:** Automated renewal debits are strictly non-refundable once processed, except under proven gateway double-charge defects identified by automated ledger reconciliation.

### 5.2 DEC-P8-02 Resolution Specification: Instructor Revenue-Share Model Evaluation

A comprehensive financial modeling, accounting reconciliation, adversarial sensitivity evaluation, and active-learner qualification semantics and fraud audit was executed and formalized in [`P8.0.3_INSTRUCTOR_REVENUE_SHARE_MODEL_EVALUATION.md`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/research/p8.0/P8.0.3_INSTRUCTOR_REVENUE_SHARE_MODEL_EVALUATION.md), [`scratch/evaluate-subscription-rev-share.mjs`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/scratch/evaluate-subscription-rev-share.mjs), and [`scratch/evaluate-qualification-semantics-audit.mjs`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/scratch/evaluate-qualification-semantics-audit.mjs):

1. **Confirmed Architectural & Financial Findings:**
   - **Double-Entry Zero-Sum Reconciliation:** Both variable pool models (Model 1: Watch-Time and Model 2: Active Enrollments) reconcile to zero-sum precision down to the single integer BDT poisha using the **Hamilton-Hare Largest Remainder Method**, satisfying `ledger_entries` invariants.
   - **Telemetry Privacy Inconsistency & Ephemeral Retention:** The preliminary qualification rule (`watch_time >= 15 min OR completed_lessons >= 1`) contained a technical contradiction: watch time originates from `learning_events`, which rotates user pseudonyms daily at `00:00:00 UTC` using an in-memory HMAC-SHA256 salt (ADR-P7-001) and deletes rows older than 90 days (`TelemetryPruningWorker`). Cross-day student watch time cannot be correlated across a month without violating ADR-P7-001.
   - **Semantics Audit of `watch_position_seconds` (P8.0.3.2):** Code inspection of [`learning.service.ts`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/apps/api/src/modules/learning/learning.service.ts) and [`VideoPlayer.tsx`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/apps/web/src/components/learning/VideoPlayer.tsx) proved that `watch_position_seconds` is strictly a playback bookmark, not an accumulator of elapsed watch time. Students can seek directly to 900s in 0 seconds, seeking backward overwrites the stored value with a smaller number, reaching $\ge 95\%$ of duration resets the playhead to 0 (ADR-0005 5.2), and lessons under 15 minutes can never reach 900s. **The assumption that `watch_position_seconds >= 900` proves 15 minutes of engagement is empirically retracted.**
   - **Evaluation of Four Qualification Alternatives:**
     - **Alternative A (Pure Completion-Based):** Qualified if $\ge 1$ lesson is completed in billing month (`lesson_progress.completed_at IN month`). **100% immune to 90-day pruning, 100% compliant with ADR-P7-001**, single indexed SQL query over persistent relational tables.
     - **Alternative B (Telemetry Watch-Time Aggregation):** Qualified if $\ge 900\text{ s}$ watch time accumulated in month. **Fails under current architecture**: violates ADR-P7-001 daily pseudonymity, requires an unhashed daily tracking table, and data is lost after 90 days.
     - **Alternative C (Corrected Relational Hybrid):** Qualified if completed $\ge 1$ lesson in month OR accessed course in month with $\ge 2$ distinct lessons visited (`COUNT(DISTINCT lesson_id) >= 2`). **100% immune to 90-day pruning, 100% compliant with ADR-P7-001**, captures both course finishers and legitimate review learners using existing relational fields.
     - **Alternative D (Enrollment + Recency Gate):** Qualified if enrolled and `enrollments.last_accessed_at IN month`. **Fails fraud resistance**: highly vulnerable to trivial enrollment click-spam and bot link-crawling.
   - **Financial Invariance Across Defensible Alternatives:** Simulation on the 100-subscriber cohort ($32,401.25\text{ BDT}$ pool) confirmed that Alternative A and Corrected Alternative C distribute revenue within 1.0% variance of each other (Web Dev: 46.8%-47.6%, Data Science: 28.2%-28.6%, UI/UX: 17.1%-17.7%, DevOps: 6.7%-7.3%), proving that eliminating telemetry watch-time tracking does not distort instructor compensation.
   - **Bot Vulnerability (Model 1 vs Model 2):** In simulation, a 10-bot video loop attack captured 98.7% of the platform pool under Model 1. Under Model 2 (Alt A or Corrected Alt C), each bot account represents at most 1 unique learner, blunting bot effectiveness by over 78%.
   - **Platform Solvency & Cash-Flow Risk (Model 3: Retainers):** Fixed retainers ($36,000\text{ BDT/month}$ across 4 instructors) expose the platform to severe cash deficits under low subscriber volumes ($-31,125\text{ BDT}$ deficit at 10 subscribers; break-even requires $\ge 75$ subscribers under corrected gateway charges).

2. **Evaluator Recommendation:**
   - **Model 2 (Threshold-Gated Active Enrollments with Alternative A or Corrected Alternative C)** is technically recommended as the baseline for Phase 8 because it is 100% immune to 90-day telemetry pruning, operates strictly on persistent ACID relational data (`enrollments` and `lesson_progress`), complies with ADR-P7-001 without schema modifications, preserves zero solvency risk, and delivers high anti-gaming resistance.

3. **Decisions Still Required from Leadership:**
   - Primary model selection (Model 2 vs Model 1 vs Model 3).
   - If Model 2 is selected: Qualification strategy selection (**Alternative A: Pure Completion** vs. **Corrected Alternative C: Completion OR Multi-Lesson Activity**).
   - Gateway fee allocation policy (absorbed by platform commission vs deducted from gross receipts).
   - Escrow hold period for subscription pool payouts (14 days vs 30 days).

4. **Authorized Owners Responsible for Resolution:**
   - **Executive Leadership** & **Head of Instructor Operations** (in coordination with Finance Lead).
   - Status remains **`PENDING`** until formal policy selection is recorded.

### 5.3 DEC-P8-03 Resolution Specification: Subscription Cancellation, Refund & Financial Policy Evaluation

A comprehensive comparative policy evaluation, regulatory and provider compatibility audit, and integer-poisha financial simulation was conducted and formalized in [`P8.0.4_SUBSCRIPTION_CANCELLATION_AND_REFUND_POLICY_EVALUATION.md`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/docs/research/p8.0/P8.0.4_SUBSCRIPTION_CANCELLATION_AND_REFUND_POLICY_EVALUATION.md) and [`scratch/evaluate-subscription-cancellation-refunds.mjs`](file:///d:/Work/2026/techsprout-main/scratch/evaluate-subscription-cancellation-refunds.mjs):

1. **Existing Platform Baseline & Non-Reusability:**
   - The existing refund engine (`apps/api/src/modules/refunds/refund-requests.service.ts`) is designed exclusively for single one-time course orders (`item_type = COURSE`), enforcing a 7-day request window and `< 20%` individual course progress.
   - Subscriptions provide catalog-wide entitlements across multiple courses; progress cannot be evaluated against an entire dynamic catalog.
   - Subscriptions pool revenues across multiple instructors (`SubscriptionRevenueService`), meaning any post-settlement refund cannot be clawed back from individual instructor wallets without triggering negative balance invariant violations (`CHECK (available_balance_cents >= 0)`) or breaching instructor trust.
   - Subscription cancellation, cooling-off refunds, and dunning lifecycle state transitions require separate, purpose-built subscription services (`SubscriptionLifecycleService`, `SubscriptionRefundService`).

2. **Verified Authoritative External Requirements:**
   - **The Consumers' Right Protection Act, 2009 (ভোক্তা অধিকার সংরক্ষণ আইন, ২০০৯ — Act No. 26 of 2009):**
     - Sections 37, 40, 44, 45, and 53 prohibit price inflation, deceptive advertising, and service failure.
     - The Act does **not** mandate a statutory right of withdrawal or cooling-off period for digital streaming services or SaaS memberships. Any cooling-off period is a **contractual product policy**.
     - Once published, policy terms are legally binding under Sections 44 and 45; arbitrary refusal to honor published refund terms constitutes an actionable statutory offense.
   - **Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines, 2021 (ডিজিটাল কমার্স পরিচালনা নির্দেশিকা ২০২১ — Gazette Notification No. 26.00.0000.112.014.004.2021-182):**
     - *Clause 3.1.4 & 3.1.5:* Platform credentials, terms and conditions, refund policies, product specifications, and total prices must be clearly disclosed prior to payment authorization.
     - *Clause 3.1.9:* Digital wallets, gift cards, or cash vouchers cannot be issued or used without Bangladesh Bank approval. Trapping customer refunds in captive platform credits is legally prohibited.
     - *Clauses 3.3.4, 3.3.6 & Section 3.5:* Approved refunds must be disbursed within **a maximum of 10 calendar days** via the **exact same payment medium/channel** (Card, bKash, Nagad) through which the student originally paid.
   - **SSLCommerz Payment Gateway Constraints & Sunk Fees:**
     - The SSLCommerz Refund API (`merchantTransIDvalidationAPI.php` with `refund_amount`) processes reversals back to the originating funding source.
     - Under Bangladesh tax regulations (VAT Act 2012), gateway commissions are subject to 15% VAT. On a ৳500 charge, the 2.875% effective fee is **৳14.38 (1,438 poisha: ৳12.50 base MDR + ৳1.88 VAT)**.
     - **Contractual Status:** TechSprout's specific contractual MDR schedule with SSLCommerz is an **unconfirmed scenario assumption** awaiting formal written addendum approval.
     - **Non-Refundability:** Gateway MDR fees and VAT are **non-refundable** upon customer refund. The platform permanently absorbs a ৳14.38 sunk cash loss per full refund.
     - Chargeback penalties: Contested card renewals incur 1,000–2,500 BDT in non-refundable dispute administration fees. Maintaining a chargeback ratio $> 0.9\%$ risks merchant account suspension.

3. **Core Policy Options Evaluated:**
   - **Cancellation Models:**
     - *Option A (Cancel-At-Period-End):* Student maintains full catalog access until `current_period_end`. No mid-cycle prorated refunds. Zero ledger adjustments mid-cycle. Clean, standard SaaS UX. Self-service one-click resume supported prior to expiration.
     - *Option B (Immediate Cancellation with Proration):* Immediate access revocation with daily/hourly proration. Rejected due to micro-transaction gateway fee losses, accounting pool instability, and complex fractional poisha math.
   - **Initial Cooling-Off Window:**
     - *Option A (48-Hour Capped Cooling-Off):* 48 hours from initial activation, $\le 2$ lessons completed across catalog, 0 certificates generated. 100% full refund (৳500.00). Platform absorbs the non-refundable ৳14.38 gateway fee as a customer acquisition cost (CAC). Instantly revokes entitlement.
     - *Option B (Strict Zero-Refund Policy):* High dispute rate, customer friction, potential consumer protection complaints.
   - **Recurring Renewal Refund Policy:**
     - *Option A (Controlled 48-Hour Unused Exception):* Zero refunds on renewals by default, with a strict automated exception: if requested within 48 hours of renewal charge AND zero lessons were accessed in the renewed billing period (`COUNT(lessons_accessed) = 0`). Full refund issued; platform absorbs ৳14.38 fee.
     - *Option B (Blanket No-Refund Enforcement):* Leads directly to bank chargebacks. Financial simulation proved that for 1,000 renewals with 10 complaints (4 resulting in chargebacks), Option B loses **-8,000.00 BDT** in chargeback penalties and lost fees, whereas Option A costs only **-115.04 BDT** in sunk gateway charges (8 x ৳14.38), saving **7,884.96 BDT** and preserving a 0.00% chargeback ratio.
     - *Defect/Duplicate Policy:* Automated 100% refund without condition for gateway duplicates or system billing glitches.
   - **Payment Failure & Dunning Strategy:**
     - 5-day grace period with 4 smart retry attempts (Day 0, Day 1, Day 3, Day 5) preserving active access. Simulation showed 75% payment recovery rate, recovering 1,500.00 BDT out of 2,000.00 BDT failed renewals.
     - Access suspended (`PAST_DUE_SUSPENDED`) immediately upon 4th failed attempt on Day 5. Permanent expiration (`EXPIRED`) on Day 14.

4. **Financial Reconciliation, Ledger Invariants & Post-Settlement Protections:**
   - Integer minor-unit poisha balancing verified across all scenarios (`scratch/evaluate-subscription-cancellation-refunds.mjs`).
   - **Direct Instructor Wallet Clawbacks are REJECTED:** If a renewal refund or chargeback occurs after monthly pool settlement, clawing back instructor wallets risks violating `CHECK (available_balance_cents >= 0)` if funds were withdrawn, creating negative balances and operational friction.
   - **Platform Subscription Reserve Account:** The platform retains 5% of its retained commission into an internal `SUBSCRIPTION_RESERVE_ACCOUNT` (৳688.41/month per 100 subscribers; ৳8,260.92/year), which absorbs all post-settlement chargebacks and dispute fees without touching settled instructor earnings.

5. **Evaluator Recommended Policy Package:**
   - **Cancellation:** Cancel-at-period-end with full access through cycle, zero mid-cycle proration, self-service one-click resume.
   - **Initial Cooling-Off:** 48-hour window from checkout, $\le 2$ lessons completed, 0 certificates, 100% refund, platform absorbs ৳14.38 gateway fee.
   - **Renewals:** Strict no-refund general rule with 48-hour unused exception ($0$ lessons accessed) and 100% automated refund on duplicate debits.
   - **Dunning:** 5-day grace period, 4 retry attempts, instant suspension on Day 5 failure, expiration on Day 14.
   - **Ledger & Reserve:** 5% platform commission reserve allocation to insulate instructor wallets from post-settlement adjustments.

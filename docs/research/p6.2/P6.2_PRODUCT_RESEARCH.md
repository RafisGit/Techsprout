## 1. Executive Summary & Research Mandate

TechSprout School LMS has successfully released and verified **Milestone P6.1 (Decoupled Event & Notification Engine + Resource Ownership Guard)** in production. With foundational zero-trust resource authorization (`ResourceOwnershipGuard`) and asynchronous notifications in place, the platform is now architecturally primed to transition from an *administrator-curated, centralized catalog* to an *autonomous, multi-tenant instructor platform*.

Currently in TechSprout:
- Instructors are forced to use administrative endpoints (`/api/v1/admin/courses`) and administrative navigation (`/admin/courses`), creating serious role confusion.
- Clicking "Overview" in `AdminNav` throws an immediate `HTTP 403 Forbidden` error because `/dashboard/admin/overview` is hardcoded to require `role === 'admin'`.
- The publishing tab displays a static placeholder message instructing educators to "request administrative review" via external channels, with no in-system submission mechanism.
- Instructors have zero visibility into their learner roster on the frontend, despite the backend possessing `GET /api/v1/admin/courses/:id/enrollments`.
- Instructors have no public profile, bio, credentials, or avatar, appearing only as a plain text string on course cards.

**The P6.2 objective:** Design an enterprise-grade, pedagogically sound, and secure **Instructor Platform** that provides educators with autonomous curriculum authoring, publication governance, learner tracking, and professional identity—while preserving institutional administrative control and absolute data integrity.

## 2. Competitive & Modern LMS Benchmark Analysis

We evaluated leading global LMS platforms (Higher Ed and Commercial Marketplaces) and local Bangladesh EdTech leaders to distill proven product patterns:

### 2.1 Canvas LMS (Instructure) — Academic & Institutional Benchmark
- **Information Architecture:** Course $\rightarrow$ Modules $\rightarrow$ Items (Pages, Discussions, Quizzes, Assignments).
- **Instructor Autonomy:** Instructors create, edit, and organize curricular content within assigned course shells. However, course creation and term assignment remain restricted to institutional administrators or department heads.
- **Publishing Workflow:** Individual modules and items have granular draft/published toggles. The overall course cannot be accessed by students until the instructor toggles "Publish Course".
- **Learner Monitoring:** SpeedGrader and Course Roster ("People") provide immediate insight into student enrollment status, submission timestamps, and grade progression.
- **Relevance to TechSprout:** TechSprout already mirrors Canvas's `Course -> Module -> Lesson -> Quiz` hierarchy. P6.2 must adopt Canvas's clean separation between administrative catalog governance and instructor pedagogical authoring.

### 2.2 Udemy & Coursera — Commercial Marketplace Benchmark
- **Instructor Portal Segregation:** The instructor workspace (`/instructor`) is completely segregated from the learner marketplace (`/`) and administrative control panels.
- **Course Quality Review Gateway:** Instructors cannot unilaterally push a course to the live public marketplace. They must submit the course to the **Course Quality Review Team** with a pre-submission validation checklist (e.g., minimum 5 lessons, HD video, complete learning objectives, passing quiz).
- **Review States:** Courses transition through a formal finite-state machine: `DRAFT` $\rightarrow$ `IN_REVIEW` $\rightarrow$ `PUBLISHED` (or returned to `DRAFT` with itemized reviewer feedback).
- **Instructor Profile & Branding:** Rich public educator profiles featuring professional headline, biographical narrative, credentials, avatar, and total student counts across all published courses.
- **Relevance to TechSprout:** TechSprout operates as a curated commercial academy. Adopting Udemy's quality review workflow ensures brand reputation and curricular rigor while preventing incomplete draft courses from leaking into the public catalog.

### 2.3 Local Bangladesh EdTech Ecosystem (10 Minute School, Shikho, Ostad, Interactive Cares)
- **Course Delivery Characteristics:**
  - Instructors are prominent industry figures (Software Architects, Competitive Programmers, Robotics Engineers) whose personal branding drives enrollment conversion.
  - High demand for transparent learner rosters: Instructors need to verify student identity, batch enrollments, and completion rates.
  - Operational model: Administrators establish course contracts and pricing; instructors build curriculum and deliver instruction.
- **Relevance to TechSprout:** TechSprout's primary audience is Bangladeshi technology learners paying in BDT via SSLCommerz. Instructor profiles must prominently display academic and industry credentials to build trust and drive course conversion.

## 3. User Personas & Problem Space

### Persona 1: The Course Instructor (Primary User)
- **Representative User:** Dr. Sarah Mitchell (Lead AI Instructor, `instructor@techsprout.edu`).
- **Context:** Subject-matter expert hired to author and teach technical courses (e.g., *Modern Web Design*, *AI with Python*).
- **Pain Points:**
  1. *Confusing Access:* Forced into `/admin` routes where administrative overview pages reject her with 403 errors.
  2. *Workflow Paralysis:* Once she finishes building modules and lessons, she cannot submit her course for platform review.
  3. *Blind Instruction:* She cannot see which students have enrolled in her courses, what progress they have made, or who completed the curriculum.
  4. *Invisible Identity:* Her name appears as plain text without bio, photo, or credentials.
- **Goal:** A dedicated, distraction-free workspace to author curricula, track student engagement, and present a professional educator profile.

### Persona 2: The Institutional Administrator (Secondary User / Governance)
- **Representative User:** TechSprout Platform Administrator (`admin@techsprout.edu`).
- **Context:** Responsible for institutional brand reputation, tuition pricing, payment reconciliation, and legal compliance.
- **Pain Points:**
  1. *Operational Bottleneck:* Currently forced to manually perform every single course edit or lesson upload on behalf of instructors.
  2. *Quality Risk:* Without an approval queue, there is no formal mechanism to audit course content before making it purchasable.
- **Goal:** Delegate curriculum authoring to instructors while retaining absolute authority over course approval, pricing, category assignment, and unpublishing/archiving.

### Persona 3: The Enrolled Student (Beneficiary)
- **Representative User:** Test Student (`student@techsprout.edu`).
- **Context:** Enrolled learner taking courses to acquire job-ready technical skills.
- **Expectation:** Needs to know who is teaching the course, verify instructor qualifications, and receive timely course updates.

## 4. End-to-End Instructor Workflow Journey

A production-grade instructor workflow comprises six sequential stages:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        P6.2 INSTRUCTOR LIFECYCLE JOURNEY                               │
├───────────────────┬───────────────────┬───────────────────┬────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Workspace      │ 2. Curriculum     │ 3. Pre-Submission │ 4. Publication             │
│    Access         │    Authoring      │    Validation     │    Review Queue            │
├───────────────────┼───────────────────┼───────────────────┼────────────────────────────┤
│ Instructor logs   │ Instructor edits  │ System checks:    │ Admin audits course:       │
│ into /instructor. │ modules, lessons, │ • >= 1 module     │ • Approve -> PUBLISHED     │
│ Views owned       │ quizzes, media.   │ • >= 2 lessons    │ • Reject -> DRAFT + reason │
│ courses & metrics.│ Guard enforces    │ • Price & thumb   │ In-app notification        │
│                   │ zero-trust IDOR.  │ Instructor clicks │ dispatched to instructor.  │
│                   │                   │ "Submit Review".  │                            │
├───────────────────┴───────────────────┴───────────────────┴────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. Post-Publishing Monitoring & Learner Roster                                         │
│ • Instructor views enrolled students, progress percentage, completion dates.           │
│ • Course edits restricted or versioned to prevent active learner disruption.           │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Stage 1: Workspace Access & Profile Management
- Instructor logs in via `/login` and is redirected to `/instructor/dashboard` (or selects "Instructor Portal" from the user menu).
- Top-level KPI cards display: Owned Courses, Active Enrolled Students, Courses in Review, and Draft Courses.
- Profile tab allows editing pedagogical bio, headline, avatar, and social/GitHub links.

### Stage 2: Course & Curriculum Authoring
- Instructor views a filtered list containing exclusively courses where `course.instructorId === currentUserId`.
- Accesses course editor with tabs: **Curriculum** (Modules & Lessons), **Quizzes** (Questions & Options), **Settings** (Metadata & Thumbnail), and **Publishing**.
- Zero-trust `ResourceOwnershipGuard` protects every sub-resource mutation.

### Stage 3: Pre-Submission Validation Checklist
- Prior to submission, an automated validation engine verifies pedagogical readiness:
  - Course title, description, and category are defined.
  - Valid thumbnail image attached.
  - At least one module with at least two lessons.
  - Valid pricing (integer minor units in BDT).
- If validation fails, blocking issues are displayed in a checklist modal.
- If validation passes, instructor confirms submission. Course transitions to `IN_REVIEW`.
- Outbox event `CourseSubmittedForReview` is persisted and enqueued.

### Stage 4: Administrative Review & Governance Queue
- Admin views `/admin/courses/review-queue` listing all courses with status `IN_REVIEW`.
- Admin reviews curriculum structure, video quality, and pricing.
- **Action A: Approve:** Course transitions to `PUBLISHED`. Outbox event `CoursePublished` emits; in-app notification delivered to instructor.
- **Action B: Reject:** Admin provides mandatory rejection feedback. Course reverts to `DRAFT`. In-app notification delivered to instructor with itemized feedback.

### Stage 5: Learner Roster & Progress Monitoring
- Instructor navigates to `/instructor/courses/:id/learners`.
- Paginated table displays: Student Name, Email, Enrollment Date, Status (`ACTIVE` / `COMPLETED`), Progress Percentage, and Certificate Awarded indicator.
- Search and status filter (`ALL`, `ACTIVE`, `COMPLETED`).

## 5. Product Assumptions & Safety Constraints

1. **No Direct Public Publishing by Instructors:** Instructors cannot unilaterally set `status = 'PUBLISHED'`. All publications require administrator review to safeguard brand reputation and legal compliance.
2. **Pricing Governance:** Administrators define or approve course prices. Instructors can propose pricing, but final commercial terms are institutional.
3. **Financial Invariants Intact:** Milestone P6.2 introduces **zero changes** to SSLCommerz payment flows, order calculation, invoice generation, or refund processing.
4. **No Multi-Vendor Split Wallets in P6.2:** Instructor revenue sharing, automated payouts, and wallet balances are explicitly deferred to Phase 7.
5. **No SCORM / LTI:** TechSprout utilizes modern native video streaming and markdown lesson text. Enterprise packaging formats are permanently out of scope.
